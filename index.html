留最好的一張，丟掉整串連拍。 Keep the best shot. Lose the burst.

Apple 的「重複項目」抓不到「手動連拍」——你按住快門連拍十幾張、它認為那是刻意拍的。snapsift 把它們揪出來，幫你只留最好的一張。 Apple's Duplicates detector leaves manual bursts alone — hold the shutter, get 15 near-identical frames, and it thinks you meant to. snapsift finds them and keeps just the best one.

為什麼用 snapsiftWhy snapsift

站在 Apple 自己的框架上，一切都在裝置裡。Built on Apple's own frameworks. All on-device.

🔒

完全在裝置上Fully on-device

PhotoKit 讀取、Vision 分析、原生刪除——沒有任何資料離開你的 Mac。PhotoKit reads, Vision analyses, native delete — nothing ever leaves your Mac.

✨

挑出真正最好的一張Keeps the genuinely best frame

讀取 Apple 自己算好的品質分數，留下最銳利、構圖最好的，而不只是檔案最大的。Reads Apple's own quality scores to keep the sharpest, best-composed frame — not just the biggest file.

😊

看臉挑張Face-aware

用 Vision 偵測臉與睜眼，連拍裡自動選大家都看鏡頭、眼睛張開的那張。Vision finds faces and open eyes, picking the frame where everyone looks their best.

🕓

跨時間找相似Cross-time look-alikes

用 Apple 神經 feature print 找出不同天存下的同一張照片，不只是當下的連拍。Apple's neural feature prints find the same photo saved on different days, not just time bursts.

⭐

最愛一律保留Favorites are sacred

最愛永遠不會被刪；影片預設略過。安全是預設值。Favorites are never deleted; videos are skipped by default. Safe by default.

↩️

隨時可復原Always recoverable

刪除走系統的「最近刪除」，30 天內隨時救回；iCloud 同步到所有裝置。Deletions go to Recently Deleted — recoverable for 30 days, synced across your devices.

開始使用Get started

不需要 Xcode，一行指令就有可雙擊的 App。No Xcode — one command builds a double-clickable app.

1 · 取得原始碼1 · Clone

git clone https://github.com/CVERInc/snapsift
cd snapsift/app

2 · 建出 App2 · Build the app

./scripts/build-app.sh        # → ~/Desktop/snapsift.app

3 · 掃描、審查、刪除3 · Scan, review, delete

雙擊 snapsift.app（首次未簽名請右鍵→打開），首次掃描會請求照片存取權。掃描 → 並排審查每群 → 刪除多餘的。Double-click snapsift.app (first time: right-click → Open). It asks for Photos access on first scan. Scan → review each cluster → delete the extras.
